Centline In progress

For a one-truck owner-operator deciding, at a loading dock at night, whether a load is worth taking.

Brokers quote dollars per loaded mile. The empty miles cost the same and pay nothing, which is why new carriers go under. Centline works out a true cost per mile and answers what a load nets after deadhead, then builds the detention clock, the invoice and the broker payment record on that same number.

My own project, still being built. Currently adding share links so a dispatcher, a driver and a broker can read one live load without an account between them, and a design system that lints its own colour contrast in CI.

About

I study informatics at Iowa, human-computer interaction, with minors in Spanish and psychology. That combination is not incidental.

Almost everything I build is for someone operating inside a language or a system that was not designed with them in mind. The design half of my training makes me ask who is on the other side of the screen. The engineering half lets me ship the answer.

The habit I would most like to be judged on is in the log: I measure before I claim, and when I am wrong I go and check rather than defend it.
