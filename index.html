About

Iowa City

I study informatics at the University of Iowa, human-computer interaction, with minors in Spanish and psychology. That combination is not incidental. Almost everything I build is for someone operating inside a language or a system that was not designed with them in mind.

The design half of my training makes me ask who is on the other side of the screen. The engineering half means I can build the answer rather than hand it off, which is why I keep ending up on the seam between the two. I care most about the decisions that get made under constraint: what got cut, what got measured, and what turned out to be wrong.

I am looking for product design and UX engineer roles starting after I graduate in May 2027, and a summer 2027 internship in parallel.
