Sollo

Internship, shipped

For a foreign resident of Japan facing a system written in a language and a format they were never the audience for.

U

CommU

Research

For a student who commutes to campus, arrives for class, and leaves again without ever finding a way into campus life.

Off-campus students described their time on campus as purely functional: arrive, attend, leave. Every one of them said they would meet people in the same situation if there were any way to do it. I ran the study, then built the web app around what it found: events in one place, posted by anyone, and a way to find the other people who drive in.

L

Component lab

Built

For a hiring manager who wants to see the interaction craft rather than read a claim about it.

A small accessible component set in React and TypeScript, with no UI library. Every colour, space and radius comes from one token file, so a single switch rethemes all of it without a component knowing it happened. The multi-select takes arrow keys, Home and End, type-ahead and Backspace, and the dialog traps focus and hands it back.

About

Iowa City

I study informatics at the University of Iowa, human-computer interaction, with minors in Spanish and psychology. That combination is not incidental. Almost everything I build is for someone operating inside a language or a system that was not designed with them in mind.

The design half of my training makes me ask who is on the other side of the screen. The engineering half means I can build the answer rather than hand it off, which is why I keep ending up on the seam between the two. I care most about the decisions that get made under constraint: what got cut, what got measured, and what turned out to be wrong.

I am looking for product design and UX engineer roles starting after I graduate in May 2027, and a summer 2027 internship in parallel.
