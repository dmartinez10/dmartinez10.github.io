How I work with Claude

The AI assistant in every project

Claude is an AI assistant. I use it to draw design options side by side and to check finished work against rules I wrote for each project.

I work this way because comparing real options leads to better decisions than debating one. The decisions stay mine, and no change goes live without my approval.
