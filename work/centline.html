Centline

A phone app for truck drivers who own their one truck: every load as one record, from saved to paid. I design it and build it, on my own.

What Centline is

It is for a driver who owns his one truck and runs it as his own business, often in his first year, reading his phone one-handed at a loading dock at night. Centline keeps each load in one place, from the day it is saved to the day it is paid: where it stands, its paperwork, and the invoice that goes out with that paperwork attached.

It is in testing and not yet in the App Store, so no one uses it for real yet.

Why I built it

My dad has driven a truck for 32 years, and he owns his own. Driving is only half of that job; the other half is paperwork, and the money waits on it. I built Centline for that half: the stretch between delivering a load and getting paid for it.

What I did

  • Every product decision, including cutting a whole half of the app when it turned out to be the wrong half.
  • The design system. Every colour comes from one set of tokens, checked for WCAG contrast, with a night palette for a loading dock at 2am.
  • Rules held by tests, not by memory. Every screen fits every phone from 280 to 440 points wide, and nothing ever grades a driver’s record.
  • Options before code. Each change is drawn two to four ways, picked, written down, then built and walked on my own phone. Claude Code is in the loop: it draws the options I choose between and checks the work against my rules.

The call I would make again

Centline began as a tool for pricing loads. Six weeks in, I cut the entire cost side, because the real pain is getting from delivered to paid. What is left does two jobs well instead of five jobs halfway: move the load, and get it paid.
